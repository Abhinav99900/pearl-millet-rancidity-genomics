# Data dictionary

## manuscript_data/variant_counts_Table1.tsv
Summary of raw and filtered SNP/INDEL variant counts for GHB-732 and GHB-538.

## manuscript_data/variant_overlap_Table4.tsv
Record-level SNP and INDEL overlap based on exact CHROM:POS:REF:ALT identity from unsplit filtered VCF records. Candidate-gene overlap is based on exact gene-ID identity.

## manuscript_data/impact_class_counts_Table3.tsv
Impact-class composition of final putative rancidity-associated candidate genes.

## manuscript_data/chromosome_distribution_Table5.tsv
Chromosome-wise distribution of common, GHB-538-unique, and GHB-732-unique rancidity-associated candidate genes.

## manuscript_data/top_candidates_Table6.tsv
Top prioritized common and genotype-unique putative rancidity-associated candidate genes selected for downstream validation.

## manuscript_data/flagstat_summary_SuppTableS5.tsv
SAMtools flagstat-based alignment QC summary for final duplicate-processed BAM files.

## heatmap_inputs/functional_category_by_impact_matrix.tsv
Functional-category by predicted-impact-class count matrix used for the function-aware mechanistic heatmap.
